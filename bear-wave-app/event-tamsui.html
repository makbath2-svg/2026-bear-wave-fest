淡江農場
暮夏浪熊祭 Tamsui Farm
Summer Bear Fest

抓住夏天的尾巴，來淡水農場吃烤肉、玩水、交朋友！ Catch the end of summer! BBQ, pool play, and make friends!

立即報名Register Now

活動亮點Event Highlights

    淡水農場擁有優美的自然景觀與寬敞舒適的烤肉場地。我們特別為浪熊們準備了豐盛的海陸BBQ，讓大家在美食與美景中輕鬆交流。
    現場當然少不了沁涼的泳池戲水！這次請各位浪熊務必預備好你最厲害、最吸睛的戰袍級泳褲，現場開放大家盡情秀出自信、展現自我！ 歡迎自備泳圈、水槍等裝備，跟著身邊的帥氣浪熊，這個最狂熱的暮夏時光一起閃耀！   Tamsui Farm features beautiful scenery and a spacious, comfortable BBQ venue. We have specially prepared a sumptuous Surf & Turf BBQ for our bears to socialize in a relaxing atmosphere.
  Of course, refreshing pool play is a must! This time, all bears must prepare your most stunning and eye-catching battle-grade swim trunks. Show off your confidence and unique self to the fullest! You are welcome to bring your own swim rings, water guns, and gear. Shine together with the handsome bears around you in this most passionate late summer season!

    • 8/16 日～9/11 日申請退款者，將酌收 200 元之手續費。
